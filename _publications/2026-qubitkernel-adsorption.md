Predicting adsorption energies is a bottleneck in catalyst discovery. We
propose QubitKernel Screening, a hybrid pipeline that maps local atomic
environments into a quantum-kernel feature space and uses a classical
surrogate model for large-scale screening. The method is evaluated on the
Q-Mol adsorption benchmark and reduces the number of expensive density
functional theory evaluations required for a fixed screening budget.
